Starter plan

Full body, 3 days a week with a rest day between (e.g. Mon / Wed / Fri). Do 3 sets of 8–12 reps each:
  1. Squats or lunges
  2. Push-ups or bench press
  3. Rows or pull-ups
  4. Overhead press
  5. Romanian deadlift or glute bridge
  6. Plank, 3 × 30 seconds
Add a rep or a little weight once all 3 sets feel solid. That's progressive overload, the thing that actually builds muscle.

History

Learn your form with light weights first. Sleep and rest days are when muscle grows. Never take steroids or SARMs.
